# One Line of Earned Pride

[![Competition](https://make.fun/badge/competition/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557)

[![Reward](https://make.fun/badge/reward/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557)](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mang-one-line-of-earned-pride-a0138557)

Entry for the make.fun bounty **One Line of Earned Pride** ($5, sponsor Mang).

`main.ts` (Node, standard library only, deterministic, no network) writes one
line of text — 1 to 240 characters — to `/output/entry.txt`:

> For a year Dani ran up the hill behind his house until his legs gave out; this morning he reached the top without stopping, and for the first time the hill looked small.

Why this line, against the judge brief (most important first):

1. **Earned pride** — a year of running until his legs gave out, then the top
   reached without stopping. The effort is felt, not claimed.
2. **Concrete scene, real person** — Dani, the hill behind his house, this
   morning. Seen happening, not summarised.
3. **Pride earned inside the line** — no "proud", no congratulation, no
   explanation. The turn in the last clause *is* the pride.
4. **Lands in one read** — the payoff arrives with the final words.
5. **Fresh** — a hill that looks small; no trophy, podium or graduation.

Run it:

```sh
node main.ts   # writes /output/entry.txt (169 characters, one line)
```
